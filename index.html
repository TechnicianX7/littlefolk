The little handbook

Build: Choose a building, tap a spot, then confirm Build here. Use the arrows to adjust. Clear site marks obstacles for your folk. For paving, tap the two ends and confirm the whole walk.
Meet: Tap a littlefolk to see their name, thoughts, and work. Tap a hut or workshop for upgrades. Move building preserves residents and goods. Connect storage adds a workshop delivery loop.
Explore: Drag to pan. Pinch or use + / − to zoom. With a keyboard, use arrows to pan, +/- to zoom, Space to pause, Escape to close.
Grow: Unlocks use all-time delivered materials, even after you spend them. Unmarked resources replenish. Land tools mark trees and stones for permanent clearing; your folk gather their materials and remove the roots. Plant trees to replenish the woodland.
Gather: Ring the lantern bell for a small evening together. Their hands keep anything they were carrying. It also happens naturally at dusk.
iPad: In Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen → Open as Web App. Let the app finish its first online load before going offline.
